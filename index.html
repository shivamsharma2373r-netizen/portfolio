I'm Shivam Sharma, a second-year B.Tech student in Electronics Engineering. From soldering my first circuit board to programming microcontrollers and designing custom PCBs, I've been driven by the thrill of making hardware work with precision and efficiency.

My academic and practical journey is deeply focused on embedded systems, analog & digital circuit design, microcontroller firmware, and IoT sensor networks. I thrive on bringing circuit schematics to life—from breadboard experimentation and SPICE simulation to completed, soldered PCB prototypes.
